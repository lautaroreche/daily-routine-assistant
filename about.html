Features

Seven sections, one routine

Everything is reached from a menu, grouped the way the shift runs: team, shift, packing and settings.

To Do

Daily tasks come back unticked every morning, weekly ones only on their day. One tap delegates a task, which stays on the list in orange until it is done.

OpenDelegatedDone

Traceability

Batch codes in Julian date format plus the supplier's code. The day can't go into the future, and every code is kept at or above the latest known batch.

DDD-SS+ / − batchkg → boxes

Handover

A guided form writes the shift report for the team chat. Process rules are built in, required fields are checked, and the header switches from estimated to final on its own.

EstimatedFinalCopy report

Stock

The daily count starts from yesterday's, shows what each count means in kilos or units, and colours products close to or below their reorder point.

CloseBelowBy place

Packing

A visual picklist of trays and boxes, every sushi tray with its photo and contents, and the packings of each client with the trays they carry.

PicklistPackingSushi

Roster

The weekly roster from a PDF or a photo, turned into images on the phone so it opens instantly, works offline and can be zoomed. It flags when it is a week old.

Settings: one source for suppliers and products

Suppliers (with unique traceability codes, colours and order) and products live in one place. A grid decides where each product appears, Traceability or Stock, and every screen reads from there, so a change is made once and shows everywhere.

Under the hood

One encrypted file, no backend

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. A small build script puts the sections and the fixed data together, and the result is encrypted before it is published.

Using it

On the phone, all shift long

It opens like a website and behaves like an app.

  1. Open the link and enter the password once; the session lasts 48 hours.
  2. Add it to the home screen from the browser menu.
  3. Move everything to another phone or computer with Export backup and Import backup in the menu.
Where does the data go? Nowhere. Day-to-day data stays in the phone's browser, and the backup is a file you share yourself. The fixed data ships inside the encrypted page.

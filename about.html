Daily routine assistant

A mobile web app I built to run my shift as Assistant Lead in the Low Risk area of a food production site. Paper notes, mental math and retyped WhatsApp messages became a few taps on the phone.

Open the app Password required: it holds internal work data.

What it does

To Do
Daily and weekly tasks that reset on their own, plus tasks added on the fly. Finished ones move out of the way.
Traceability
Morning picking for production with batch codes in Julian date format, supplier codes, quantities handed over and a kg-to-boxes calculator.
Handover
A guided form that writes the shift report for the team's WhatsApp group, checking required fields and switching from estimated to final at report time.
Stock
Daily count with unit equivalences and reorder points that turn products yellow or red before they run out.
Packing
Photo catalogue of every sushi tray and its contents, and a picklist of trays and boxes with size references.
Roster
The weekly roster, readable and zoomable on the phone.

How it's built

One self-contained HTML file in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with no backend. Each section is an isolated module, and the fixed data (tasks, items, photos) lives in a separate base file that is merged into the app at build time.

Everything entered day to day stays on the phone. The published app is encrypted with AES-256, so the public repository only holds ciphertext.
